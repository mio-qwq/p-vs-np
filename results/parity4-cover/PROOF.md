# Exact finite checkpoint: unrestricted parity-four pair-cover cost is five

Date: 2026-10-17. This is a reproducible finite computational proof, independently checked by a different enumeration algorithm. Historical novelty has not been established. It gives no asymptotic circuit lower bound or P versus NP separation.

## Definition and theorem

Let T be the odd-parity points of {0,1}^4, and order the negative carrier as V=(0,3,5,6,9,10,12,15). For x in T define M_i(x)={y in V: y_i=x_i}, i=0,...,3. Both literals of every coordinate are supplied as generators.

A semifilter F is an upward-closed family of subsets of V containing V and not containing the empty set. It is above x if every M_i(x) belongs to F. A pair (A,B) of arbitrary subsets of V defeats F if A,B belong to F and A intersection B does not. A pair-cover defeats every semifilter above at least one x in T. Its unrestricted minimum cardinality rho is exactly 5.

These are the finite set-theoretic definitions in Complexity/Computability/Circuit/Fusion/Semifilter.lean, complexitylib2 commit ffc377b51c1faac475b8cb5376d658fec457e70a. The PairCover.ofCircuit theorem supplies the circuit-to-cover direction; the upper bound below is also verified directly by finite closure, so this checkpoint does not require importing a Lean build.

## Upper bound: five actual AND gates

For coordinate bits a,b,c,d use

- u=(a OR b) AND ((NOT a) OR (NOT b));
- ub=(a OR (NOT b)) AND ((NOT a) OR b);
- v=(c OR d) AND ((NOT c) OR (NOT d));
- vb=(c OR (NOT d)) AND ((NOT c) OR d);
- output=(u OR v) AND (ub OR vb).

Here u and v are the respective two-bit XORs, and ub and vb their complements. The output is u XOR v. There are five binary AND gates; all remaining gates are ORs or supplied input literals. verify_upper5.py verifies all sixteen truth assignments.

The five gate-input truth-set pairs, restricted to V and encoded as eight-bit subset masks in the stated order, are (190,125), (215,235), (252,63), (207,243), (60,195). For each x, start with the upward closure of its four M_i(x). Whenever both members of one listed pair are accepted, insert its intersection and all supersets. The script verifies that the empty set is generated for each of the eight x. A surviving above-x semifilter would contain every set generated by this induction, contradicting rejection of the empty set. This independently proves that the five pairs cover all semifilters, not merely the 120 witnesses used below.

## Exact lower bound: finite witness certificate

frozen_witnesses.json contains 120 explicit 256-bit acceptance masks. Bit S records whether subset mask S belongs to the family. The checker validates every family from the original definition: empty rejection, full-set acceptance, upward closure, and acceptance of all four M_i(x) for some positive x. Thus rejecting all four-pair covers of this finite witness set suffices for the unrestricted lower bound.

The first 64 families carry positive integer weights; the remaining 56 have weight zero. The weighted families are reconstructed independently from these formulas:

1. Eight canonical families F_x(S) iff some M_i(x) is contained in S, weight 24 each.
2. Thirty-two families F_R(S) iff |S intersection R|>=2, where R is a three-element subset of V containing no complementary pair, weight 15 each.
3. Twenty-four hitting families F_R(S) iff S intersection R is nonempty, where R is V with a Hamming-distance-two pair removed, weight 10 each.

The total weight is 912. All 32,896 unordered pairs A<=B are enumerated, and each defeats weight at most 240. Consequently three pairs cannot cover even the weighted witnesses. This integer certificate also gives the fractional bound 912/240=19/5.

For any hypothetical four-pair cover, the following necessary conditions follow without optimization software:

- Every chosen column has weight at least 912-3*240=192.
- The union of any two chosen columns has weight at least 912-2*240=432.
- The intersection of any two chosen columns has weight at most 4*240-912=48. Indeed its weight is at most the total multiplicity excess of the four columns over their union.

A column records which of the 120 witnesses a pair defeats. There are 2,503 eligible pairs of weight at least 192, and 587 distinct eligible columns. Identical columns are interchangeable for this finite cover problem. Repeating a column cannot help; a cover using fewer than four distinct columns is already excluded by the weighted bound. Therefore it suffices to enumerate four distinct columns satisfying the pairwise necessary conditions.

The compatibility graph has 31,513 edges. check_frozen_four.py exhaustively searches increasing-index compatible tuples, pruning only when uncovered weight exceeds 240 times remaining slots, an uncovered witness has no remaining covering column, or the last available slot cannot cover every missing witness. Its complete run visits 1,904 nodes with 731 coverage prunes and finds no cover.

Independently, the parent checker reconstructed the acceptance columns and enumerated ALL compatible four-cliques without these witness-based DFS prunes: 85,161 triangles and 53,226 four-cliques, zero covers. The packaged implementation is check_four_cliques.py and its result is four_clique_result.json. This distinct algorithm checks the conclusion rather than trusting the first search's negative result. A second independent auditor loaded only the explicit 120 masks, verified the original semifilter conditions and all pair weights, and used a rarest-uncovered-witness exact-cover algorithm: 184 recursive calls, no four-pair cover. That auditor also independently verified the five-AND truth table and full closure upper-bound proof. The packaged rarest-witness implementation is independent_verify.py; its actual run is recorded in independent_result.json.

## Reproduction and trust boundary

Python standard library only:

    python verify_dual.py
    python verify_upper5.py
    python check_frozen_four.py

The last checker reads frozen_witnesses.json, not the discovery history or solver output. It reconstructs the weighted prefix, checks every frozen witness, enumerates the complete pair universe, verifies all exact weight inequalities, and runs the exhaustive finite search. A time-budget exception produces an inconclusive status rather than a proof. The recorded run completed in under one second.

Discovery used SciPy linear programming and mixed-integer programming, plus a least-semifilter closure separation oracle. Their infeasibility reports are not trusted in this proof. Discovery scripts and history are retained for provenance but are not verifier dependencies. Frozen masks are explicit mathematical objects; their validity is rechecked regardless of their origin.

## Why the initial witness recipes do not scale

These are separately proved obstructions to particular fractional-dual recipes, not upper bounds on arbitrary covers.

If V contains complementary points y and NOT y, the single pair (V minus {y}, V minus {NOT y}) defeats every canonical F_x. Each true-literal match set contains exactly one of y and NOT y; because x differs from each negative point, there is a match set excluding each one individually, but no match set excludes both. Thus any feasible dual supported on canonical families has total weight at most 1.

For any hitting family with |R|>=2, a uniformly random bipartition (A,B) of V defeats it with probability 1-2^(1-|R|)>=1/2. Hence any feasible dual on these hitting families has total mass at most 2.

For any two-of-three majority family, independently color every carrier point I,P,Q with probability 1/3 each and use A=I union P, B=I union Q. The family is defeated exactly when its three points receive all three colors, probability 6/27=2/9. Hence feasible dual mass on these majority families is at most 9/2.

Partitioning overlaps arbitrarily, any feasible dual supported on the union of these three classes has mass at most 1+2+9/2=15/2, independently of dimension. This stops that specific scalable recipe. The additional 56 closure witnesses and integral cover incompatibilities close the finite four-variable case; no dimension-uniform lower-bound invariant has been proved.
