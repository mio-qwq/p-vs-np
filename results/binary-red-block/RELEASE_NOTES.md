# Release notes: binary red-block checkpoint

Prepared 18 October 2026; local-only and additive.

- Adds a sufficient all-order nonvanishing theorem for exact-count fibers on complete bipartite graphs when all red edges lie in a proper nonsingular square binary block.
- Gives the exact degree and leading coefficient for distinct real offsets and distinct nonnegative integer exponents, including nonprincipal row/column sets and unordered exponent lists.
- Supplies the parity-balanced, fully supported n=6, k=3, t=1 example with first surviving signed coefficient 6 at degree 12.
- Records the corrected full-support boundary and adds a separate theorem for complete all-red square blocks of size k<=floor(n/2), including singular all-ones incidence blocks. It explains the degree drop in the all-red 2x2 example without extending the nonsingular formula beyond its hypotheses.
- Includes three unchanged mathematical checkers, explicit finite certificates, independent proof reviews, normal/optimized execution checks, raw-Laplace cross-checks, and certificate/file corruption controls.
- Omits duplicate optimized output and generated caches. All internal artifact links are relative.

The written proof establishes only the sufficient family specified in [README.md](README.md). Finite verification is corroboration, not a substitute for that proof. No general ASNC, P-versus-NP, historical-novelty, human-peer-review or Lean claim is made. Existing results and releases are untouched.
