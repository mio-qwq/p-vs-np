# Verified auxiliary results: 18 October 2026

This source-only research release contains two bounded results:

- [Fixed-depth affine-hull obstruction](fixed-depth-hull-obstruction/README.md): for every fixed conditioning depth, an infinite family of complete bipartite binary-labeled matching instances defeats the specified conditioned affine-hull relaxation. The proof is general; a depth-five instance has two independent exact finite implementations.
- [Four-row consecutive-exponent theorem](monic-four-consecutive/README.md): every matching-supported binary 4-by-4 mask gives a nonzero shifted-power determinant for distinct real offsets and consecutive exponents. An independent symbolic audit covers every labeled mask and rejects corrupted certificates.

These are auxiliary results and an obstruction to one algorithmic route. They do not solve P versus NP, validate other papers, or settle arbitrary-size/gapped-exponent conjectures. Novelty is not established. The independent audits are AI-assisted and are not human peer review or proof-assistant verification.

## Reproduction

Python 3.10+; standard library only. From the repository root:

    python3 results/verify-20261018.py

The verifier checks `manifest-20261018.json`, runs in clean temporary copies, generates the monic certificate before the independent audit, checks generated hashes, and executes negative controls. See `verification-20261018.json` for the recorded publication run. The general derivation and producer checkers remain byte-identical. The compact release intentionally omits the large monic certificate and audit-output JSON files. They are generated only during reproduction and checked against expected hashes in `monic-four-consecutive/GENERATED.json`; the retained small fixed-depth certificates remain byte-identical. Editorial report/audit changes and the verifier's matching report hash are explicitly recorded. Original archive SHA-256 values and member-level provenance are recorded with each result. Archives, caches, transient logs, and unrelated draft investigations are intentionally omitted.
