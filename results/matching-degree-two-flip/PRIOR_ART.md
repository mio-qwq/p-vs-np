# Prior-art context and unresolved novelty

The closest checked locality precedent is [Dafni–Filmus–Lifshitz–Lindzey–Vinyals, Theorem 3.2 and section 4.2](https://yuvalfilmus.cs.technion.ac.il/Papers/measures.pdf). In the perfect-matching domain with changed-edge metric |M0 \ M|, the Boolean sensitivity-based bound and beta_c >= 1/3 give radius 6s+3. Their degree framework is real. This is relevant precedent, but it is not directly the all-field degree bound min(n,2d+1) stated here.

[Rhoades, Theorem 3.16 / Remark 3.17](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.08718v2) supplies permutation shadow-junta bases, including a commutative-ring formulation; it is not itself a small-ball evaluation theorem. [Liu–Ma–Rhoades–Zhu, Theorem 4.4](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~mol008/Paper2.pdf) concerns the complex graded matching coordinate ring. [Filmus–Lindzey, Theorems 1.1 and 3.3](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2022/59.pdf) gives real harmonic presentations. These are algebraic context, not asserted proofs of the exact radii in this packet.

The focused source checks did not establish an earlier identical exact all-field radius theorem. That does not establish historical novelty or exclude an equivalent formulation. Priority and literature equivalence remain unresolved. No novelty assertion is made.
