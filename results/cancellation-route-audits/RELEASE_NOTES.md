# Cancellation-route checkpoint notes

This additive checkpoint collects square complement reductions, a standard-label rectangular top-coefficient cancellation with a certified nonzero next coefficient, five-row one-partial-row necessary conditions, and two exactly excluded symmetric candidates.

The scope is narrower than a general nonvanishing theorem. No zero ASNC polynomial, full five-row proof, general ASNC result, or complexity separation is claimed. See [README](README.md) and the [current-status note](STATUS.md) before interpreting historical source wording.

All seven original checkers are unchanged. The dependency-free aggregate reruns exact finite arithmetic and integrity controls; `--full` additionally reruns symbolic identities and independent direct determinants with installed SymPy. Full symbolic verification is never implied by a default-only pass.

The package is portable, hash-inventoried, and intended only for additive paths under `results/cancellation-route-audits/`. Its preparation does not publish, modify an existing release, or contact a remote service.
