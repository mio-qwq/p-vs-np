# Status at this checkpoint

The original [five-row proof](five-row/PROOF.md) is preserved byte-for-byte. Two historical statements there have been superseded:

1. Its opening says the derivation is not independently audited. The later [independent audit](five-row/AUDIT.md) now approves the stated necessary conditions and candidate exclusions. This is an AI-assisted mathematical/computer-algebra audit, not human peer review or proof-assistant verification.
2. Its final residual-branch section calls the `d=9`, exponent-set `(0,5,9,12,14)` example unresolved. The subsequent [exact exclusion](next-candidate/RESULT.md), independently checked in that audit, rules it out. Under `209v^4−1226v^2+209=0`, the first surviving tested moment is 12, with exact degree 28 and a rigorously nonzero remainder at either allowed positive value of `v`.

These updates do not settle other residual branches. The general five-row mask problem and even the complete one-partial-row classification remain unresolved here.

The [rectangular obstruction](tied-block/RECTANGULAR_OBSTRUCTION.md) has a canceled candidate leading term but nonzero next terms in every feasible target. Calling it a zero polynomial or ASNC counterexample would be incorrect. The result rules out reliance on that leading-sum nonvanishing route alone.

The [prior small-square proof](tied-block/SMALL_SQUARE_BACKGROUND.md) is retained as historical background. Its old statement that tied sums are outside its scope is resolved for square blocks by the new [complement/tied proof](tied-block/PROOF.md); it is not a claim that the present checkpoint leaves square ties open.
